# Feedback

This playbook is read-only, so changes to the content are made by the author only. You are welcome to comment, though.

## What you can share

- **Missing topics:** a concept from the roadmap that you expected to find here
- **More questions:** interview questions you have seen, with the section they belong to
- **Errors:** a wrong fact, broken link, or unclear explanation
- **General comments:** what helped you, and what did not

## How to leave feedback

Comments are posted in the **[Discussions](https://github.com/AthiraMohan14/Complete-Java-Resource-PlayBook/discussions)** area of the repository. You need a free GitHub account to post.

When you comment, please include:

1. The page or section you are referring to
2. What you found, or what you would like added
3. A short example, if one helps

Your comments do not change the playbook directly. The author reviews them and decides what to add.
