# Behavioural Questions

Answer templates and guidance for questions about how you work. Every template must be adapted to your own experience. Do not memorise someone else's story, and do not claim events that did not happen.

---

## Topics

### [STAR method](star-method.md)
**Situation - Task - Action - Result - metrics - story bank - follow-ups**
How to structure a behavioural answer, with a clearly marked sample to replace.

### [Leadership and teamwork](leadership-and-teamwork.md)
**Influence - mentoring - decisions - knowledge sharing - prioritisation - remote teams**
Templates for questions about leading without authority and working well with others.

### [Conflict and failure](conflict-and-failure.md)
**Disagreement - feedback - mistakes - incidents - ownership - learning**
Templates for questions about tension, setbacks, and what you changed afterwards.

### [Career motivation](career-motivation.md)
**Why this role - strengths - weaknesses - growth - team fit - goals**
Templates for questions about your direction, your motivation, and what you are learning.
